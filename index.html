Next Fool City

A Completely Unnecessary Concept

Under Completely Unnecessary Construction

Next Fool City is an online town populated by AI-run characters from the novels and stories of Rick West (primalbeet). Visitors walk in, wander the streets, and talk with residents who have their own histories, relationships, and lives that keep going whether anyone is watching or not. The city sits on the island of Marengo Mango, across the Bay of Mango from Fool City, founded by Herbert Washington, George's brother, who built a raft and floated over. It's under construction now, built in public through numbered Transmissions on Substack and YouTube.
